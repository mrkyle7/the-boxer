Your aim stays where you last set it. A block only covers the height you're aiming at, so guard the head and a tummy kick goes straight in. Head shots hurt more; tummy shots knock the wind out and drain stamina. Every attack costs stamina, a guard with none left breaks open, and hitting someone mid-attack is a counter for 40% more.
