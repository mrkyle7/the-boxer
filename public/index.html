The Boxer

Two fighters. Three rounds. One of you walks out with the belt.

How to fight
Move
A D or arrow keys
Block
hold S, ↓ or Shift
Jab
J fast, long reach, light
Hook
K slower, hurts, chips through a guard
Uppercut
L short range, huge damage, cracks guards
Slip
Space dodge everything for a moment

Every punch and slip costs stamina. Blocking drains it too, and a guard with no stamina left breaks open. Hitting someone mid-punch is a counter and does 40% more.
